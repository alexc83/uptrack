# My Changes Aren't Saving

If you're having trouble saving information in UpTrack, the problem may be related to missing information, invalid entries, an expired session, or a temporary connection issue.

Follow the steps below to identify the problem and try saving your changes again.

## Check for missing or invalid information

UpTrack requires certain information before you can save a credential or continuing education (CE) record.

If a field is missing or contains an invalid value, the form may display an error message.

Before trying again:

1. Review the form for highlighted fields or error messages.
2. Complete any required fields.
3. Check that dates, numbers, and email addresses are entered correctly.
4. Select **Save Changes**, **Save Credential**, or **Save CE Record**, depending on the form.

## Check credential information

If you're adding or editing a credential, confirm that:

- All required fields are completed.
- You've selected **License** or **Certification**.
- The expiration date is valid.
- The renewal cycle is entered as a whole number of months.
- Required CE hours are not negative.

If your credential doesn't require continuing education hours, enter **0**.

## Check continuing education information

When saving a CE record, confirm that:

- You've entered the activity title and provider.
- CE hours are greater than zero.
- The completion date is valid.
- You've selected an associated credential when creating the record.

If you're uploading a certificate, wait for the upload to finish before saving the CE record.

## Check your internet connection

If UpTrack cannot communicate with the server, your changes may not save.

Try the following:

1. Confirm that your device is connected to the internet.
2. Check whether UpTrack displays a connection error.
3. Try saving again once your connection is restored.

Avoid repeatedly selecting Save while a request is still processing.

## Your session may have expired

If your session has expired, UpTrack may require you to log in again.

You may see:

**"Your session expired. Please log in again."**

Log in again and return to the record you were editing.

**Important:** Unsaved changes may be lost when your session expires. You may need to reenter your information.

## The record may have changed

Occasionally, a record may no longer be available or may have changed since you opened it.

If UpTrack reports that a record cannot be found or that the request conflicts with stored information:

1. Return to the **Credentials** or **CE Records** page.
2. Refresh the page.
3. Find and reopen the record.
4. Review its current information before making changes again.

If the record was deleted, you'll need to create a new record if you still need to track that information.

## Confirm your changes were saved

After saving, return to the relevant page and check that your updated information appears.

For credentials and CE records, you can select the record to review its details.

For account information, UpTrack displays **"Profile updated"** when your changes are saved successfully.

If you're unsure whether a save completed, check the existing record before trying to create another one. This can help avoid duplicate entries.
