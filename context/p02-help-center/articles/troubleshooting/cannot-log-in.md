# I Can't Log In

If you're having trouble logging in to UpTrack, there are a few common issues you can check.

Follow the steps below to identify the problem and try logging in again.

## Check your email address and password

If you see the message **"Invalid email or password"**, UpTrack couldn't verify the information you entered.

Try the following:

1. Make sure you're using the email address associated with your UpTrack account.
2. Check your password for typing errors.
3. Confirm that Caps Lock isn't enabled.
4. Select **Log in** to try again.

If you've recently changed your email address or password, make sure you're using your updated information.

## Too many login attempts

If you see **"Too many requests. Please try again later."**, UpTrack has temporarily limited additional login attempts.

Wait a while before trying again.

Avoid repeatedly submitting the login form while the limit is in effect.

## Unable to connect to UpTrack

If you see **"Unable to reach the server right now. Please try again."**, UpTrack may be experiencing a connection problem.

Try these steps:

1. Check that your device is connected to the internet.
2. Refresh the page.
3. Try logging in again.
4. If the problem continues, try again later.

## Your session has expired

If you've previously logged in, you may see:

**"Your session expired. Please log in again."**

This means your previous session is no longer valid.

Enter your email address and password to start a new session.

If you've recently changed your password, use your new password when logging in.

## Forgot your password?

UpTrack does not currently offer a self-service password reset or account recovery option.

To change your password through the application, you must be logged in and know your current password.

If you've forgotten your password and cannot log in, there is currently no password recovery option available within UpTrack.
