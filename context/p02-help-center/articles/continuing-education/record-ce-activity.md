# Record a CE Activity and Link It to a Credential

Use CE records to track completed learning activities and associate the hours with a credential.

## Before you begin

Add at least one credential. Each CE record must belong to a credential.

## Record a CE activity

1. Open **CE Records**.
2. Select **Add CE Record**.
3. Select the associated credential when prompted.
4. Enter the activity **Title** and **Provider**.
5. Enter the number of **Hours** completed (greater than zero).
6. Enter the **Date completed**.
7. Optionally choose a certificate file and wait for its successful upload.
8. Select **Save CE Record**.

The recorded hours contribute to the total for the credential you selected.

## Attach supporting documentation

You may attach one certificate to the CE record. The file uploads when selected, but you must still save the CE record to associate it with the activity. Supported formats and limits are covered in the certificate-upload article.

## Important to know

UpTrack sums the CE hours associated with a credential, regardless of completion date. It does not automatically decide which hours count toward a particular renewal period. Confirm eligibility with your issuing organization.
