# View, Edit or Delete CE Records

The CE Records page helps you review and manage your completed continuing education activities.

## Find a CE record

1. Open **CE Records**.
2. Use search to find an activity by title or provider.
3. Filter by credential or by whether a certificate is on file.
4. Select a record to see its details.

The list displays ten records per page when enough records are present. CE records are organized by completion date.

## Edit a CE record

1. Open the CE record.
2. Select **Edit CE Record**.
3. Update the title, provider, hours, completion date, or certificate.
4. Select **Save Changes**.

To associate an activity with a different credential, create a new CE record under that credential rather than changing its credential from the edit form.

## Delete a CE record

1. Open the record you want to remove.
2. Select **Delete CE Record**.
3. Review and confirm the deletion.

Deleting the record removes its hours from the associated credential's CE total.

## What happens to attached certificates?

If you delete a CE record that has an UpTrack-managed certificate, the certificate is also scheduled for removal from storage.

UpTrack handles this cleanup automatically, although deletion from storage may take additional time.

Before deleting a CE record, save a separate copy of any certificate you may need later.
