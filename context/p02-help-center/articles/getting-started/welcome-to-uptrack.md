# Welcome to UpTrack

UpTrack helps you organize professional credentials, document completed continuing education (CE), and keep supporting certificates with the CE activities they belong to.

## What you can do

- **Track credentials.** Add licenses and certifications, record expiration dates, and see which credentials need attention.
- **Record CE activities.** Add courses and completed learning activities to a specific credential and track the hours you've entered.
- **Attach certificates.** Keep one supporting file with each CE record and view it from the record details.
- **Review your dashboard.** See credential counts, upcoming expirations, CE hours still needed, and recent CE-related activity.
- **Print a CE report.** Open the report for a particular credential and use your browser's Print function.

## Start here

Create an account, add your first credential, and then record CE activities against that credential. You can attach a certificate while creating or editing a CE record.

## Important to know

UpTrack helps you organize the information you enter. It does not automatically verify licensing or certification requirements with issuing organizations, and the renewal cycle does not automatically calculate an expiration date. Always check renewal requirements with your licensing or certification authority.
