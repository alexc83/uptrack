# Add Your First Credential

Adding a credential is the first step toward organizing your CE activities in UpTrack.

## Add your credential

1. Log in to UpTrack.
2. Open **Credentials**.
3. Select **Add Credential**.
4. Enter the credential's name and select **License** or **Certification**.
5. Enter its expiration date and issuing organization.
6. Enter the renewal cycle in months and the required CE hours. Enter **0** if no CE hours are required.
7. Select **Save Credential**.

## What happens next

Your credential appears in your credentials list. Its status depends on the expiration date you entered. You can now create CE records associated with that credential.

## Remember

Enter the expiration date shown by the issuing organization. UpTrack does not calculate the next expiration date from the renewal cycle, and it does not verify the organization's requirements for you.
