# Create an Account and Log In

Create an UpTrack account to save and manage your credentials and CE records.

## Create an account

1. Open the UpTrack sign-up page.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, and **Password**.
3. Submit the registration form.

After successful registration, UpTrack signs you in automatically.

## Log in

1. Open the UpTrack login page.
2. Enter your account email and password.
3. Select **Log in**.

## If you cannot log in

Check your email and password for typing errors. If you changed your email address or password, use the updated details. UpTrack currently does not offer self-service password recovery.
