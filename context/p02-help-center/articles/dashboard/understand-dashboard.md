# Understand Your Dashboard

The dashboard gives you a quick view of your saved credentials and CE activity.

## Credential overview

The summary cards show **Total Credentials**, **Active**, **Expiring Soon**, and **Expired** based on the expiration dates you entered.

## Upcoming Expirations

This section highlights credentials expiring soon, generally within the next 90 days, including credentials that expire today. Already-expired credentials are not shown here; use the **Credentials** status filter to locate them.

## CEs Still Needed

This section highlights credentials with required CE hours greater than zero when the recorded total is below the required amount. CE totals include the activities associated with the credential; UpTrack does not automatically restrict them to a renewal period.

## Recent Activity

Recent Activity summarizes CE-related activity, such as recorded CE completions and certificate uploads. It is not a complete audit log of every account or profile change.

## If you need more detail

Open the relevant credential or CE record to review its full information, or use the CE report for a printable summary.
