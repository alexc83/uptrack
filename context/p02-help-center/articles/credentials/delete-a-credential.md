# Delete a Credential

Deleting a credential removes it from UpTrack along with the CE records associated with it.

## Before you delete

Review the credential and any linked CE activities. Save copies of reports and certificates you may need, because deleted records cannot be restored from the UpTrack interface.

## Delete a credential

1. Open **Credentials**.
2. Select the credential you want to remove.
3. Select **Delete Credential**.
4. Review the confirmation message and confirm the deletion.

The credential and its linked CE records will no longer be available in UpTrack.

## What happens to attached certificates?

When you delete a credential, UpTrack also removes its associated continuing education records.

Certificates uploaded and managed by UpTrack are scheduled for deletion from storage as part of this process.

File cleanup may not happen immediately. If the storage service is temporarily unavailable, UpTrack automatically retries the deletion.

**Important:** Deleting a credential and its associated records cannot be undone. Save copies of any certificates you may need before deleting the credential.
