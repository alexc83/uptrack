# View, Search and Edit a Credential

Your Credentials page helps you find, review, and update the licenses and certifications you've saved.

## Find a credential

1. Open **Credentials**.
2. Use the available search or status filters to narrow the list.
3. Select a credential to review its details.

You can use status filtering to locate expired credentials, which do not appear in the dashboard's Upcoming Expirations section.

## Edit a credential

1. Open the credential you want to change.
2. Select its **Edit** action.
3. Update the information that has changed.
4. Save your changes.

Check the updated expiration date carefully. UpTrack uses the stored date—not the renewal cycle—to determine the credential's status.

## CE activities and reports

CE records linked to the credential contribute to its displayed CE total. You can open the credential's CE report to review the activities you entered.
