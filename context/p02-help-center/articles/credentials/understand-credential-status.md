# Understand Credential Status and What Needs Attention

UpTrack uses the expiration date entered for each credential to show whether it is active, approaching expiration, or expired.

## Credential status

- **Active:** The expiration date is more than 90 days away.
- **Expiring Soon:** The expiration date is today or within the next 90 days.
- **Expired:** The expiration date was before today.

Status updates are based on the date stored in UpTrack. The application does not independently verify the credential with its issuing organization.

## Find credentials needing attention

Open your **Dashboard** to review **Upcoming Expirations**, which shows credentials approaching expiration. If additional credentials are approaching expiration, select the option to see more expirations.

To find credentials that have already expired, open **Credentials** and use the status filter. Expired credentials are not included in the dashboard's Upcoming Expirations section.

## Understand CE progress

UpTrack adds the CE hours recorded against a credential. It does not automatically exclude activities based on the current renewal period, so review the activity dates and your organization's renewal requirements yourself.
