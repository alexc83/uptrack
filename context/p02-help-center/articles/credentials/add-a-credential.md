# Add a Credential

You can add licenses and certifications to track their expiration dates and related continuing education.

## Add a credential

1. Open **Credentials** and select **Add Credential**.
2. Enter the **Credential Name**.
3. Select a credential type: **License** or **Certification**.
4. Enter the **Expiration Date**.
5. Enter the **Issuing Organization**.
6. Enter the **Renewal Cycle** as a number of months.
7. Enter **Required CE Hours**. Use **0** when no hours are required.
8. Select **Save Credential**.

All six pieces of information are required. The renewal cycle must be a positive number of months, and CE hours cannot be negative.

## How expiration dates work

UpTrack uses the expiration date that you enter to determine credential status. The renewal cycle is recorded for reference and does not automatically change or calculate an expiration date.

## Next step

Once a credential is saved, you can associate CE activities with it.
