# View an Uploaded Certificate

You can view certificates you've attached to your continuing education (CE) records directly from UpTrack.

Uploaded certificates are associated with individual CE activities, making it easier to find supporting documentation when you need it.

## Find an uploaded certificate

1. Log in to UpTrack.
2. Select **CE Records** from the navigation menu.
3. Find the CE activity associated with your certificate.
4. Select the record to open its details.
5. Locate the **Certificate** section.

If a certificate is attached, you'll see an option to view it.

You can also use the **Certificate** filter on the CE Records page and select **On file** to display records with attached certificates.

## Open a certificate

1. Open the CE record containing your certificate.
2. In the **Certificate** section, select **View certificate**.
3. Your certificate will open in a new browser tab, where you can review the document.

Depending on your browser and the file format, you may also have options to print or save a copy.

## How certificate access works

UpTrack uses protected access for newly uploaded certificates.

When you select **View certificate**, UpTrack checks that you're authorized to access the CE record and provides a temporary link to open the file.

You don't need to manage these links yourself. Simply return to the CE record and select **View certificate** whenever you need to access the certificate again.

## If your certificate doesn't open

If you're having trouble viewing a certificate:

- **Check your browser's pop-up settings.** UpTrack opens certificates in a new tab, so your browser may need permission to open it.
- **Try opening the certificate again.** Temporary access links expire, but UpTrack can generate a new link when you select View certificate.
- **Confirm you're logged in.** Your UpTrack session must be active to request access to protected certificates.
- **Check your internet connection.** A connection problem may prevent the certificate from loading.

If the CE record shows **No certificate uploaded**, the record does not have an attached certificate available through UpTrack.

## Important to know

Each CE record supports one attached certificate.

UpTrack does not currently provide a dedicated certificate download button. Your browser may offer options to save or print an opened file.

If you download a copy to your device, that copy is stored outside UpTrack and is no longer protected by UpTrack's access controls.
