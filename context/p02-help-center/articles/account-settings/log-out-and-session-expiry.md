# Log Out and Session Expiry

Your UpTrack session lets you access your credentials and CE records while you're signed in.

## Log out

When you're finished, use the application's **Log out** action to end your current session. This is especially important if you're using a shared computer.

## Session expiry

If your session is no longer valid, UpTrack may ask you to log in again. Enter your account email and password to start a new session.

Your session may also end after a password change. Any information you have not saved may need to be entered again.

## If you have trouble signing back in

Check that you're using the correct email and password. See the login troubleshooting article for other common problems.
