# Update Your Account Information

You can update the name and email address associated with your UpTrack account.

## Update your profile

1. Log in and open **Settings**.
2. Find your profile information.
3. Update your **Name** or **Email**.
4. Select **Save Changes** when your changes are complete.

UpTrack confirms when your profile has been updated.

## If your email address cannot be saved

Check that the address is correctly formatted. If another account already uses that email, UpTrack may ask you to choose a different address.

If you change your email address, use the new address the next time you log in.

## What you cannot change here

The profile settings currently let you edit your name and email address, not your password. Use the separate password-change option for your password.
