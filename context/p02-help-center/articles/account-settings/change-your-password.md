# Change Your Password

You can change your UpTrack password while you're logged in and know your current password.

## Change your password

1. Log in and open **Settings**.
2. Open the **Change Password** option.
3. Enter your **Current Password**.
4. Enter a **New Password** and repeat it in the confirmation field.
5. Submit the password change.

The new password must meet the application's password rules. The form checks that the new password and confirmation match and that the new password differs from the current one.

## After you change it

After a successful password change, UpTrack signs you out. Log in again using your new password.

## If you forgot your current password

UpTrack does not currently provide a self-service password reset or recovery email. You cannot use the password-change form without your current password.
